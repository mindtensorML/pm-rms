AI-Assisted Value Portfolio Pilot

A five-week test of where a frontier model helps a value team, and of how the team will know.

v2.2, October 2026Mapped against the CFA Institute Code and Standards (2024) and NIST AI RMF 1.0 with its Generative AI ProfileA general method with no client data

The pilot builds a value sleeve for a hypothetical endowment from public data only. It runs from mandate to a mock investment committee. A pilot is only worth running if it can fail, so the measures, baselines and pass rules are fixed in week 1, before any model output exists. Model drafts are checked against their sources and against dates the market could actually see. Entry decisions, the portfolio sign-off and the committee decision stay with people. The color tags are design estimates of what a model could do with the owner accountable, not measured results. Measuring them is the point of the pilot.

Who decides

Every step has an owner, and 9 roles cover them all. The pilot lead decides whether the model passed its test before research starts. The PM makes every entry, exit and weighting decision and signs every memo. The mock IC chair records the committee's decision, and the CIO gives the pilot's verdict against rules set in week 1. If the portfolio still misses a limit after one rework, the CIO decides whether to accept the shortfall or stop. A model can run a step or draft it. It never decides. Where no standard speaks to a step, it is marked practitioner practice (22 of 92 steps).

  • Model draftsA model drafts, the owner reviews and signs30
  • Model-runA model executes, the owner is accountable and samples the output35

Context

The five weeks

Week 1 sets the rules and the data. Weeks 2 to 4 build the portfolio, and week 5 judges the pilot. The replay of stages 6 and 7 is optional. By default it runs after week 5. It can run alongside weeks 4 and 5 only on a cut-down set with a second analyst.

Evaluation design

How the pilot is judged

The CIO fixes these in MD-05, before week 2. The pass rules shown are proposals for the CIO to confirm or change. Any later change goes in the decision log with a reason. Hit rate is reported but cannot carry the verdict, because about 20 names over a few months cannot show skill.

  1. Thesis quality

    Blind score from one to five on six criteria. Variant view, valuation support, dated catalysts, testable kill criteria, traceable sources, fact kept apart from opinion.

    Baseline
    Analyst-only memos on the same three to five names
    Pass rule
    On the paired names, model-assisted memos average no lower than the analyst-only memos, and no criterion averages below three
    CFA Code V(A), V(B); Practitioner practice

  2. Source accuracy

    Share of sampled claims the cited passage supports. All valuation figures plus 20 other claims per memo.

    Baseline
    Error rate in the analyst-only memos
    Pass rule
    No unsupported figure in any signed memo
    NIST AI 600-1 MS-2.5-003

  3. Library accuracy

    Correct and correctly cited answers on the gold question set, with the held-back half scored apart.

    Baseline
    The same set run in week 1
    Pass rule
    At or above the MD-05 threshold and the week 1 score on the held-back half, and no invented citations
    NIST AI 600-1 MP-2.3-001

  4. Review vigilance

    Share of seeded errors the reviewer catches before signing.

    Baseline
    None. This is an absolute check
    Pass rule
    All seeded errors in valuation figures are caught
    NIST AI 600-1 s2.7

  5. Time saved

    Logged hours from watchlist name to signed memo, including review and correction.

    Baseline
    Analyst-only hours on the same names
    Pass rule
    A saving the CIO names in week 1, with quality no worse
    Practitioner practice

  6. Hit rate

    Share of positions whose dated catalyst or KPI moved the way the thesis said, and share beating the benchmark. Week 5 comes days after sign-off, so this needs the replay or a later review.

    Baseline
    The benchmark and the plain value screen portfolio
    Pass rule
    Descriptive only. About 20 names over months cannot show skill
    Practitioner practice

  7. Portfolio risk

    Tracking error, factor exposures, largest position, top ten weight, days to trade, stress drawdown.

    Baseline
    Mandate limits set in MD-03
    Pass rule
    Every limit met at sign-off
    CFA Code III(C)(2)

  8. Look-ahead

    Sources dated after the as-of date, and answers that reveal later events.

    Baseline
    None. This is an absolute check
    Pass rule
    Zero dated breaches. Any knowledge leak labels the replay contaminated
    Glasserman and Lin 2023
Controls

Checks on model output

Two failures can quietly ruin a pilot like this. A model can state a fact or cite a passage that does not exist. And a replay can use information nobody had on the day. These checks guard against both.

  1. Every claim cites a dated source

    Uncited claims are struck before review. A quote comes from the filing text itself, never from a summary or a search snippet.

    NIST AI 600-1 MS-2.5-003

  2. Numbers come from code, not prose

    Ratios, valuations and factor exposures are computed in code the analyst can audit.

    CFA SoPH V(A) p.131

  3. Dates follow EDGAR acceptance

    A filing exists for the pilot from the time the SEC accepted it, not from its period end or its filing date. One accepted after the 4 p.m. ET close, or on a day the market is shut, counts from the next trading day.

    Form 10-K and 10-Q General Instructions; SEC Accessing EDGAR Data; Reg S-T 13(a)(2); NYSE hours

  4. Universe as of the date

    Index membership and delisted names as they stood on the as-of date. Undated items stay out, and a source that cannot serve a window is shown as unavailable.

    Russell v7.2 s4.2.3, s4.3.1; Practitioner practice

  5. Model cutoff before the replay

    Web search stays off. If the cutoff falls inside the replay, results are labeled possibly contaminated.

    Glasserman and Lin 2023

  6. Seeded errors

    Known mistakes planted in drafts test whether the reviewer reads or rubber-stamps.

    NIST AI 600-1 s2.7

  7. Everything logged

    Model version, prompts, inputs, outputs and every edit.

    CFA SoPH p.153

  8. Replay labeled theoretical

    Never joined to actual performance.

    GIPS 2020 1.A.27

Week 1

Mandate and Evaluation Design

What good looks likeThe mandate's limits fit together, and the pass rules are written down before any model output exists.
Standards · CFA Code III(C); NIST AI RMF MEASURE 1.1, MANAGE 1.1; NIST AI 600-1 MS-2.3-002
  1. MD-01Human-onlyCIO

    Define the client profile

    A hypothetical endowment with $500M in assets and a $50M value sleeve. Write down the return objective, risk tolerance, horizon, liquidity needs and any restrictions.

    CFA Code III(C)(1)(a)
  2. MD-02Human-onlyCIO

    Set the benchmark and return target

    The v1 target is Russell 1000 Value plus 200 basis points a year. State whether that is gross or net of fees and over what horizon. The universe reaches into small caps, so Russell 3000 Value fits better. If the client keeps Russell 1000 Value, the small-cap tilt is an active bet that needs its own risk budget.

    Russell v7.2 s6.10.1, s8.2.1; Practitioner practice
  3. MD-03Human-onlyCIO

    Set position limits and the risk budget

    No more than 5 percent in one name. Add a tracking error budget, a drawdown tolerance and a liquidity rule, because Stage 5 tests against them. The liquidity rule names the share of average daily volume a trade may take and the most days a position may need to trade. A 5 percent cap needs at least 20 names to be fully invested. Otherwise the mandate must say where the rest of the money sits.

    CFA Code III(C)(2); CFA SoPH p.144
  4. MD-04Human-onlyCIO

    Set factor targets

    Value exposure (HML) of at least 0.40 and size exposure (SMB) of at least 0.25 against the Fama French factors. Name the estimation window. Say whether the point estimate or the lower end of a stated confidence band must clear each target, since with about 20 names the two can disagree. A size tilt against a large-cap benchmark needs the CIO's explicit approval.

    French Library, three and five factor descriptions
  5. MD-05Human-onlyCIO

    Pre-register the measures and pass rules

    Fix the measures in the scorecard on this page, with a pass rule for each, before week 2. Mark which rules are core, since a missed core rule stops the pilot. Set the gold-set score the model must reach before week 2. Any later change needs the CIO's written reason in the decision log.

    NIST AI RMF MEASURE 1.1, MANAGE 1.1
  6. MD-06Human-onlyPilot lead

    Set the baseline

    Fixed now and run in weeks 2 and 3, once the watchlist exists. Three to five watchlist names are drawn at random. An analyst who never sees the model's work researches them without a model, to the same memo template, and logs hours. The same names also get a model-assisted memo, so the comparison is like for like. Two portfolio baselines sit beside it. One is the benchmark. The other is a plain value screen portfolio.

    NIST AI 600-1 MS-2.3-002; Practitioner practice
  7. MD-07Model draftsCompliance officer

    Confirm data rights and keep out inside information

    A model drafts the list of every source and its licence terms. Transcripts and news feeds often limit storage and model use. The model provider's terms must keep pilot inputs out of its training. Compliance confirms that only public information enters the pilot.

    CFA AI 2022 Exhibit 4; NIST AI RMF MAP 4.1
  8. MD-08Human-onlyPilot lead

    Record the working settings

    The pilot lead writes down the settings later steps rely on, before week 2. These are the daily spot-check sample in RS-08, the news look-back in RS-06, the correlation threshold and window in DB-04, and the fee rate for replay returns. Each goes in the decision log. A change after week 1 needs a logged reason, like any pass rule.

    CFA SoPH p.153; Practitioner practice

Week 1

Universe and Dated Data

What good looks likeEvery document carries the date the market first saw it, and the model passes a test before anyone relies on it.
Standards · Russell v7.2 s4.2.3, s4.3.1, s6.10.1; SEC Forms 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K; SEC Accessing EDGAR Data; Reg S-T 13; NYSE hours; CFA SoPH pp.131, 153
  1. DA-01Model-runPilot lead

    Open the decision log

    It runs from day one. Record the model version, prompts, inputs and outputs, and every human edit and decision, so any result can be traced and rerun.

    CFA Code V(C); CFA SoPH p.153
  2. DA-02Model-runData engineer

    Start from the Russell 3000 as of the pilot date

    Use index membership on the pilot's as-of date, not today's list. Russell reconstitutes in June and December and adds IPOs each quarter.

    Russell v7.2 s4.2.3, s4.3.1
  3. DA-03Model-runData engineer

    Keep US common stock in the Russell 3000 size range

    The Russell 3000 covers companies ranked 1 to 3,000. It holds only the larger micro-caps, since the Russell Microcap runs from 2,001 to 4,000.

    Russell v7.2 s6.10.1
  4. DA-04Model-runData engineer

    Confirm public data for each name

    Drop names that lack the filings or prices the screens need, and record why.

    Practitioner practice
  5. DA-05Model-runData engineer

    Load SEC filings from EDGAR

    Pull 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K filings within the SEC's fair access limits and with a declared user agent. Earnings releases arrive as 8-K Item 2.02 exhibits.

    Form 10-K; Form 10-Q; Form 8-K Item 2.02; SEC Accessing EDGAR Data
  6. DA-06Model-runData engineer

    Load four quarters of earnings call transcripts

    Only from a source whose licence allows storage and model use. Transcripts are not SEC filings.

    NIST AI RMF MAP 4.1; CFA AI 2022 Exhibit 4
  7. DA-07Model-runData engineer

    Load prices and financial data

    Prices, share counts and reported financials. XBRL data from the SEC's own APIs, plus a market data source whose licence permits this use.

    SEC Accessing EDGAR Data
  8. DA-08Model-runData engineer

    Load news and material events

    Material events come mostly from 8-K filings. Any news feed must be licensed for model use.

    Form 8-K; Practitioner practice
  9. DA-09Model draftsData engineer

    Stamp every document with its as-of date

    Use the EDGAR acceptance time, not the period end and not the filing date. The market closes at 4 p.m. ET, or earlier on a half day. A filing accepted after the close, or on a weekend or exchange holiday, counts from the next trading day. EDGAR gives a filing the same day's filing date if it starts by half past five in the evening ET, so the filing date alone would let an after-close filing in a day early. A 10-K is due 60 to 90 days after year end and a 10-Q 40 to 45 days after quarter end, by filer size, and many arrive earlier. Amendments and restatements get their own date.

    Form 10-K Gen. Instr. A(2); Form 10-Q Gen. Instr. A.1; SEC Accessing EDGAR Data; Reg S-T 13(a)(2); NYSE hours
  10. DA-10Model draftsData engineer

    Split and index the documents

    Chunk each document and keep its source, section and as-of date on every chunk, so every answer can cite all three.

    NIST AI 600-1 MS-2.5-005
  11. DA-11Model draftsPilot lead

    Test the model on a gold question set

    Analysts write about 50 questions with known answers from the filings. Half are held back from whoever tunes the library. Score the model's answers and citations before week 2, and again in week 5.

    CFA SoPH V(A) p.131; NIST AI 600-1 MP-2.3-001
  12. DA-D1Decision gatePilot lead

    Fit to use in week 2?

    The pilot lead compares the gold-set score with the threshold set in MD-05. A miss allows one fix and retest. A second miss stops the pilot before any research depends on the model.

    CFA SoPH V(A) p.131; NIST AI RMF MANAGE 1.1

Week 2 · Stage 1

Screening and Watchlist

What good looks likeScreens run in code on dated data. The model can write and explain the code, but never does the arithmetic in prose.
Standards · French Library value sorts; Russell v7.2 s8.2.1; CFA SoPH V(A) p.131
  1. SC-01Human-onlyPortfolio manager

    Fix the screen definitions and thresholds

    Five value measures. Price to earnings, price to book, EV to EBITDA, free cash flow yield and dividend yield. Thresholds are set within each sector, since raw ratios pile value names into a few sectors.

    French Library (B/M, E/P, CF/P, D/P sorts); Russell v7.2 s8.2.1; EV/EBITDA and FCF yield are Practitioner practice
  2. SC-02Model-runQuant analyst

    Run the five valuation screens

    Computed in code on as-of data across the whole universe. The output lists each name's rank on each measure.

    CFA SoPH V(A) p.131
  3. SC-03Model draftsResearch analyst

    Apply quality tests

    Free cash flow, leverage and dividend coverage tests to weed out value traps. A model applies them and drafts the reasons. The analyst checks the borderline cases.

    Practitioner practice
  4. SC-04Model draftsPortfolio manager

    Review the watchlist

    About 30 to 50 names, with the sector spread and a first factor check. The PM can add or strike names, with the reason logged.

    CFA SoPH V(A) p.130

Week 2 · Stage 2

Research Dossiers and Sourced Library

What good looks likeEvery answer the library gives points to a dated passage in a named document.
Standards · SEC Form 10-K Items 1, 1A, 7, 7A, 8; Form 10-Q Part I; NIST AI 600-1 s2.2, MS-2.5-003
  1. RS-01Model-runResearch analyst

    Set up a dossier for each name

    One template per name, so dossiers can be compared and checked the same way.

    Practitioner practice
  2. RS-02Model-runResearch analyst

    Extract from the 10-K and 10-Q

    Business, risk factors, management's discussion, market risk and the financial statements, each with its citation.

    Form 10-K Items 1, 1A, 7, 7A, 8; Form 10-Q Part I Items 1 to 3, Part II Item 1A
  3. RS-03Model-runResearch analyst

    Summarize four quarters of earnings calls

    What management said, what analysts asked and what changed from quarter to quarter.

    Practitioner practice
  4. RS-04Model-runResearch analyst

    Extract management commentary and guidance

    Quote guidance word for word with its date. Guidance is the company's opinion, and the dossier marks it that way.

    CFA SoPH p.145
  5. RS-05Model draftsResearch analyst

    Map competitors and peers

    The model proposes peers from the filings. The analyst confirms them, because the peer set drives the comparables valuation.

    CFA SoPH V(A) p.130
  6. RS-06Model-runResearch analyst

    Log material news and events

    Dated events from 8-K filings and licensed news over the look-back set in MD-08, each tied to the name it affects.

    Form 8-K
  7. RS-07Model-runResearch analyst

    Build the sourced research library

    A searchable store of every document, with links between companies. Answers must cite document, section and as-of date. v1 called this a knowledge graph.

    NIST AI 600-1 MS-2.5-003, MS-2.5-005
  8. RS-08Human-onlyResearch analyst

    Spot-check dossier claims each day

    A second analyst checks a random sample of claims against their sources, at the sample size set in MD-08. Every miss goes in the decision log.

    NIST AI 600-1 s2.2, MS-2.5-003

Week 3 · Stage 3

Bull and Bear Debate

What good looks likeThe bear case gets the same effort as the bull case, and a person decides which names go forward.
Standards · CFA Code V(A); CFA SoPH p.132
  1. DB-01Model-runResearch analyst

    Draft the bull case

    Built only from the sourced library, with citations.

    CFA Code V(A)
  2. DB-02Model-runResearch analyst

    Draft the bear case

    Same rules. It must include the outcomes a buyer would least like to see.

    CFA SoPH p.132
  3. DB-03Model-runResearch analyst

    Find the strongest counterarguments

    The points each side has not answered, ranked by how much they would move the value.

    Practitioner practice
  4. DB-04Model draftsQuant analyst

    Check correlation risk across names

    Names that share a driver, such as one commodity or one customer, are flagged as a cluster. Return correlation uses the threshold and window set in MD-08.

    Practitioner practice
  5. DB-05Human-onlyPortfolio manager

    Review the debate

    The PM reads both cases and the open points for every name.

    CFA Code V(A)
  6. DB-D1Decision gatePortfolio manager

    Advance to a full thesis?

    About 20 to 25 names go forward. Twenty is the minimum that can fill the sleeve under a 5 percent cap, and only at equal weights, so Gate 1 rejections fall back on the completion rule in MD-03. The reason for each drop is logged.

    CFA Code V(A); Practitioner practice

Week 3 · Stage 4

Thesis Memos

What good looks likeThe model writes the first draft. The PM owns every number, every view and the kill criteria.
Standards · CFA Code V(A), V(B)(5); CFA SoPH pp.131-132, 144-145; NIST AI 600-1 s2.7
  1. TH-01Model draftsResearch analyst

    Draft the memo from the library

    The model drafts each section with citations and marks facts apart from opinions and estimates.

    CFA Code V(B)(5); CFA SoPH p.145
  2. TH-02Model draftsPortfolio manager

    Summary and recommendation

    The model drafts the summary. The recommendation is the PM's alone.

    CFA Code V(A)
  3. TH-03Model draftsResearch analyst

    Valuation

    A DCF and comparables with bull, base and bear cases, in a spreadsheet or code the analyst can audit. The model proposes inputs and cites where each came from. Value per share uses diluted shares, counting stock compensation, options and buybacks.

    CFA SoPH pp.131-132
  4. TH-04Model draftsResearch analyst

    Catalysts and timeline

    Dated events that would show the thesis working or failing. Each is an estimate and is labeled so.

    CFA SoPH p.145
  5. TH-05Model draftsPortfolio manager

    Risk register and kill criteria

    The thesis rests on two to four pillars, and each states what would prove it wrong. Kill criteria must be dated and measurable, such as a margin floor by a stated quarter. The PM commits to them before Gate 1, and the replay watches them.

    CFA SoPH p.144
  6. TH-07Human-onlyPilot lead

    Seed test errors in sample drafts

    The pilot lead plants three known errors in some drafts without telling the reviewer. The catch rate shows whether review is real or a rubber stamp.

    NIST AI 600-1 s2.7
  7. TH-06Human-onlyPortfolio manager

    PM edits and signs each memo

    The PM challenges the assumptions, sets conviction and signs. Edit time is logged for the scorecard.

    CFA Code V(A), V(C)
  8. TH-08Human-onlyPilot lead

    Clear the seeded errors

    The pilot lead records which planted errors the PM caught. The PM is then told of any missed, corrects them and signs again. No planted error reaches Gate 1, the portfolio pack or the source-accuracy check, so the vigilance test does not distort the accuracy test.

    NIST AI 600-1 s2.7; Practitioner practice

Week 4 · Stage 5

Portfolio Construction

What good looks likeWeights meet the mandate, every position can be traded at its size, and the PM signs the result.
Standards · CFA Code III(C)(2); CFA SoPH pp.132, 144; French Library
  1. PC-01Model-runQuant analyst

    Load the approved names

    With each memo's conviction, kill criteria and liquidity data.

    Practitioner practice
  2. PC-02Model draftsPortfolio manager

    Set starting weights from conviction

    The model proposes weights from the PM's conviction scores. The PM sets them.

    CFA Code III(C)(2)
  3. PC-03Model-runQuant analyst

    Estimate factor exposures

    Value and size loadings for the weighted portfolio, built from each holding's past returns against the Fama French factors over the window the mandate names, with their standard errors. The holdings' book-to-price and size confirm the direction. Profitability exposure is shown as well, since the quality tests create one.

    French Library, factor descriptions
  4. PC-04Model-runQuant analyst

    Check the factor targets

    A test in code against the mandate's value and size targets.

    CFA Code III(C)(2)
  5. PC-05Model-runQuant analyst

    Adjust weights within the limits

    An optimizer moves weights inside the position limits and the risk budget, then the targets are tested again. After three passes with no feasible weights, the shortfall is recorded and the risk checks run anyway.

    Practitioner practice
  6. PC-06Model draftsQuant analyst

    Check liquidity and how each name is held

    Days to trade each position at the share of average daily volume set by the MD-03 liquidity rule. Every name is held directly in a separately managed account. v1 routed large caps to an ETF, which cannot express a single-name thesis.

    CFA SoPH p.144
  7. PC-07Model draftsQuant analyst

    Stress test on history

    Run the portfolio through 2008 to 2009, the 2020 drawdown and 2022. Names without that history need a stated proxy.

    CFA SoPH p.132
  8. PC-08Model draftsQuant analyst

    Check drawdown and tracking error against the mandate

    Against the limits set in MD-03.

    CFA Code III(C)(2)
  9. PC-09Model draftsQuant analyst

    Check correlation under stress

    How much the positions move together in the stress periods, by cluster.

    Practitioner practice
  10. PC-11Model draftsPortfolio manager

    Challenge the position sizes

    A risk challenge kept apart from the thesis challenge at Gate 1. It covers only the positions the PC-06 to PC-09 results single out, such as the largest risk contributors or the slowest to trade. A model argues for a larger and a smaller size on each, citing those results. The PM answers each point in the pack. It is a challenge, not a vote.

    CFA SoPH pp.132, 144; Practitioner practice
  11. PC-10Model draftsPortfolio manager

    Assemble the portfolio pack

    Positions and weights, the signed memos and a risk report with factor exposures, concentration, liquidity and drawdown. Any recorded shortfall and the sizing challenge go in too.

    CFA Code V(B)(3), V(B)(4)
  12. PC-D1Decision gatePortfolio manager

    Sign off the model portfolio?

    The PM signs only if every MD-03 limit and MD-04 target is met. Otherwise the portfolio goes back once to reweight. A miss that remains after that, or at the end of week 4, goes to the CIO.

    CFA Code III(C)(2)
  13. PC-D2Decision gateCIO

    Accept a mandate shortfall?

    The CIO either accepts the portfolio with the shortfall written in the decision log, so the portfolio-risk rule records a miss, or stops the pilot. Limits are not loosened after the fact.

    CFA Code III(C)(2); NIST AI RMF MANAGE 1.1

For reviewers

Open questions for practitioners

The design rests on some judgment calls. These are the ones where a PM or CIO who has run an AI pilot can improve it most. The current split is 19 human-only, 30 model-drafted and 35 model-run steps, with 8 gates.

  1. Should the whole pilot run as of a past date?

    Then week 5 could score hit rates on real outcomes. The cost is a higher risk that the model already knows what happened, which MN-01 tries to control.

  2. Is a three-to-five-name analyst baseline enough?

    A fairer test might need more names or a second analyst, which a five-week pilot may not afford.

  3. Which pass rule should decide the verdict?

    Time saved, memo quality and source accuracy can disagree. Should one failed rule stop the pilot on its own?

  4. Carry 20 names or allow a completion sleeve?

    Under a 5 percent cap, and with which benchmark. Russell 3000 Value, or Russell 1000 Value with the size tilt as an approved active bet?
